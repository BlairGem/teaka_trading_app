Session boundaries and unavailable capabilities

One shared USDT account per user; strategy-owned positions. One dataset and one forward-only clock per process. Replacing data after any replay activity or rewinding history requires a fresh process. Another user's held exposure blocks advancing the clock; the owner must process every intervening candle. Finite replay liquidates held positions at its end. Manual orders use the last completed candle price, which is historical, not a current market quote.

Unavailable: live feeds, order book, public market trades, FX conversion, futures, WebSockets, ML training/prediction, MATLAB, additional technical analysis, manual signal creation and signal cancellation. No fitted model or live performance claim. Core SMA/EMA/RSI/MACD indicators are available.
